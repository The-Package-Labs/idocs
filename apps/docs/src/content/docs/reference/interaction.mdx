## The canvas

<dl class="keys">
  <dt>Drag</dt>
  <dd>Pan the diagram.</dd>
  <dt><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + scroll</dt>
  <dd>Zoom. A plain scroll moves the page, so a diagram never traps the reader.</dd>
  <dt>Two-finger pinch</dt>
  <dd>Zoom on a touch screen. One finger scrolls the page.</dd>
  <dt>− and +</dt>
  <dd>Zoom out and in, between 0.15× and 3×.</dd>
  <dt>Fit</dt>
  <dd>Fit the whole diagram in the frame. It also refits on its own when the frame changes size, until the reader pans or zooms.</dd>
  <dt>Drag the bottom edge</dt>
  <dd>Resize the viewer in the page, from 220 px up. A grip marks the edge. With the edge focused, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> move it by 24 px, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> by 120 px, <kbd>Home</kbd> makes it as short as it goes and <kbd>End</kbd> as tall as the window. Double-click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> puts it back. The diagram refits to the new size until the reader has panned or zoomed.</dd>
  <dt>Expand the viewer</dt>
  <dd>Make the viewer most of the window's height, keeping it in sight. The same button shrinks it again. Unlike full screen, the page stays around it.</dd>
  <dt>Full screen</dt>
  <dd>Take the whole component, with its side panel, to full screen. The resize edge and the expand button give way to it.</dd>
  <dt>Hide planned</dt>
  <dd>Appears when the diagram has planned items. Hides planned nodes and their edges.</dd>
  <dt>Follow</dt>
  <dd>Appears while a scenario is selected. Moves the camera to wherever the action is.</dd>
</dl>

## Boxes and lines

<dl class="keys">
  <dt>Hover or focus a box</dt>
  <dd>Highlights the box and everything it connects to, and fades the rest.</dd>
  <dt>Hover a line</dt>
  <dd>Highlights the line and its two ends. With an interfaces table, the matching row lights up too.</dd>
  <dt>Click a box, or <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> on a focused one</dt>
  <dd>Opens the drawer: the box's documentation, connections, links and source files.</dd>
  <dt>A link in the drawer</dt>
  <dd>Moves to the connected box.</dd>
  <dt><kbd>Esc</kbd></dt>
  <dd>Closes the drawer. Clicking the background closes it too.</dd>
</dl>

Every box can be reached with <kbd>Tab</kbd>, and has an accessible name that includes its title and subtitle.

## Nested diagrams

A box with an **Expand** and an **Open** strip on its bottom edge holds a whole diagram. See [nested diagrams](../../format/nested-diagrams/).

<dl class="keys">
  <dt>Click the box</dt>
  <dd>The side panel shows the diagram it holds as a picture, with the same two buttons beside it.</dd>
  <dt><strong>Expand</strong> icon on the box, or in the side panel</dt>
  <dd>Shows the inner diagram in place, as a group around it, and moves the camera there. <strong>Collapse</strong> in the group's header folds it back. A node inside can be expanded too.</dd>
  <dt><strong>Open</strong> icon on the box, on the group's header, or in the side panel</dt>
  <dd>Moves into the inner diagram, with its own views, scenarios and player. The icons on the box are for the pointer; the buttons in the side panel have names for the keyboard and for screen readers.</dd>
  <dt>Back, or <kbd>Esc</kbd></dt>
  <dd>Goes up one level. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the drawer first when it is open, and does nothing while focus is in something else on the page.</dd>
  <dt>A name in the trail</dt>
  <dd>Goes straight to that level and closes the ones below it.</dd>
</dl>

The level you leave is kept exactly as it was: its view, scenario, step, open drawer and expanded groups. Whatever was playing pauses. The box you came out of pulses once.

## The side panel

A panel beside the picture holds the scenarios, the timeline and the views, with the player docked at the bottom. See [the side panel](../../scenarios/playback/#the-side-panel).

<dl class="keys">
  <dt>Scenarios tab</dt>
  <dd>A card for each scenario. Pick one to play it. **Overview** goes back to the plain diagram. A scenario that a reader selects starts playing, unless the system asks for reduced motion, and the panel moves to its timeline.</dd>
  <dt>Timeline tab</dt>
  <dd>The steps of the scenario in progress, beside the picture. It keeps the step in progress in view.</dd>
  <dt>Views tab</dt>
  <dd>Switch between slices of the model. Appears when a diagram has more than one view.</dd>
  <dt><kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> on a tab</dt>
  <dd>Move to the next tab.</dd>
  <dt>The arrow at the top of the panel</dt>
  <dd>Fold the panel to a rail that keeps Previous, Play and Next, or bring it back.</dd>
  <dt>Flow, Sequence, Story</dt>
  <dd>Switch how the selected scenario is drawn.</dd>
  <dt>CC</dt>
  <dd>Turn the subtitles, the step written out over the picture, on or off.</dd>
</dl>

## The player

<dl class="keys">
  <dt>Play, Pause, Resume, Replay</dt>
  <dd>One button whose label says what it will do.</dd>
  <dt>Previous, Next</dt>
  <dd>Step through one step at a time.</dd>
  <dt>Scrubber</dt>
  <dd>Drag to any moment. Playback pauses.</dd>
  <dt>Speed</dt>
  <dd>0.5×, 1× or 2×.</dd>
  <dt>A step in the timeline</dt>
  <dd>Click, or <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>, to play that step. The path already travelled stays lit.</dd>
</dl>

## Links and the address bar

The address follows the scenario and step as `#<diagram>=<scenario>.<step>`, and the boxes opened below a diagram as `#<diagram>~=<node>/<node>`, so a link opens exactly where it was copied. See [playback](../../scenarios/playback/) and [nested diagrams](../../format/nested-diagrams/#deep-links).

## Accessibility

- Boxes are focusable buttons, and the drawer takes focus when it opens.
- Opening a nested diagram moves focus to its **Back** button, and coming back returns it to the box that was opened. A box that holds a diagram says so in its accessible name.
- Controls have labels, the player is a labelled group, and the panel's tabs are a real tab list that the arrow keys move along. The subtitles repeat what the timeline says, so they are hidden from screen readers.
- Scenarios start paused for readers who ask for reduced motion. They can still step and scrub.
- Colour is never the only signal: planned items are dashed and tagged, lookups and errors have their own line styles in the sequence view.
