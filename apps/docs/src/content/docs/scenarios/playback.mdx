Everything a reader does with a scenario happens in a panel beside the picture, so the story and the picture are in view together.

<Diagram name="checkout" scenario="place-order" />

## The side panel

The panel has tabs, and the player is docked at the bottom of it whichever tab is open.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Scenarios** | A card for each scenario of the view: its title, how many steps and phases it has, the first lines of its summary, and a small bar showing how the phases divide up. **Overview** shows the diagram on its own. |
| **Timeline** | The scenario in progress: its summary, then every step grouped by phase. Appears once a scenario is chosen, and opens by itself when one is. |
| **Views** | A card for each view. Appears when the diagram has more than one. |

The arrow at the top folds the panel away to a thin rail that keeps **Previous**, **Play** and **Next**, so the picture can have the whole width. A page can start it folded with `sidebar={false}`. When the widget is narrow, under about 820 px, the panel moves above the picture instead of beside it.

## The player

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play, Pause, Resume, Replay** | Runs the scenario. The button's label says what it will do next. |
| **Previous step, Next step** | Moves one step at a time. If a step is half played, **Next** finishes it first. |
| **Scrubber** | Drag to any moment. Playback pauses while you scrub. |
| **Speed** | 0.5×, 1× or 2×. |
| **CC** | Turns the [subtitles](#subtitles) on and off. |
| **Flow, Sequence, Story** | Switches how the scenario is drawn. See [story](../story-view/) and [sequence](../sequence-view/). |

The player also shows where you are: `step 3/9 · 0:04`.

## The timeline

Every step is listed in the **Timeline** tab, grouped by phase, beside the picture. The panel scrolls by itself to keep the step in progress in view, and never scrolls the page to do it. The step in progress is highlighted, finished steps are marked, and notes appear under their step. A multi-hop step names the nodes it passes through ("via CDN, API gateway").

**Click a step** to play it. The path already travelled stays lit, so a reader can jump to the middle of a story and still see how it got there. The list is also keyboard-friendly: Tab to a step and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>.

## Subtitles

While a scenario plays, the step in progress is written out over the bottom of the picture: its number, what it does, who talks to whom, and the note you wrote for it. They are on by default, and **CC** in the player or on the picture's toolbar turns them off. A page can start without them with `subtitles={false}`. Beside a sequence or a story they stay at the bottom of the window as the page scrolls.

## Follow

On a large diagram the whole picture is small, so the camera can follow the action. A **Follow** button appears while a scenario is selected. Following starts on automatically when the diagram is wider than 1500 px or taller than 950 px, and stops as soon as the reader pans or zooms. Press it again to bring it back.

## Links to a moment

The address bar follows the scenario and step, so readers can share exactly what they are looking at:

```text
https://example.com/examples/checkout/#checkout=place-order.4
```

The format is `#<diagram>=<scenario>[.<step>]`, where the step is the number shown in the timeline. A page with several diagrams joins them with `&`:

```text
#checkout=place-order.4&payments=pay.2
```

Opening such a link selects the scenario, switches to its view, and shows the step already played, paused, so the reader decides when to continue.

A link can also open [nested diagrams](../../format/nested-diagrams/#deep-links): `#shop~=orders/stock` opens the `orders` node, then the `stock` node inside it. The scenario of the diagram at that level is keyed by the diagram names that lead to it, joined with `/`: `#shop/orders/stock=reserve.3`.

<Aside type="note">
  The address updates when playback stops, not on every frame. Embeds that should not touch the address, such as a decorative demo, can pass `hash={false}`. See [the Diagram component](../../embed/diagram-component/).
</Aside>

## Autoplay and reduced motion

A scenario a reader **clicks** starts playing. One that a page selects with `scenario="..."` waits, unless the page also passes `autoplay`:

```mdx
<Diagram name="checkout" scenario="place-order" autoplay />
```

Readers who ask their system for reduced motion are never autoplayed, and clicking a scenario selects it without playing. They can still press **Play**, step through, or scrub.

## How it works

A scenario compiles to a **timeline**: for every step, when it starts and ends, and for every hop, when the packet is on which edge. `frameAt(t)` is a pure function that turns a time into a picture: which packets are where, which nodes are lit. The player is only a clock that advances `t`. That is why scrubbing, stepping, deep links and the tests are all exact. See [how it works inside](../../project/architecture/).
